Approach

How Kamb builds

The four companies are not a collection of unrelated interests. They are four positions on the same work: building and running the systems a business actually operates on.

The work comes first

ParallaxAi and Assay House are in real businesses every week. That is where the group learns which problems recur and which are one-offs.

Products come out of the work

A problem that turns up often enough, in enough businesses, becomes something built once and used many times rather than solved again each time.

What earns its place becomes a company

LAUNCHT and Proving Grounds both began inside the service work before becoming companies of their own.

The group holds, it does not merge

Each company keeps its own name, market and identity. Kamb owns them and sets the long view; it does not run their brands.

The production layer

The operating companies run an automated delivery layer: research, drafting, publishing, reporting and monitoring run as scheduled machinery rather than as billable hours, with a human checkpoint before anything reaches a client. It is why a small group produces the volume it does, and it is documented rather than described.

What the companies build on

Named plainly, because it is useful to know and because calling a supplier a partner would be misleading. These are platforms and models the operating companies buy and build with.

Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI for language models. Supabase for data and authentication. n8n for workflow automation. Shopify, WordPress and Google for the surfaces clients already own.

Where a formal partner status exists it is stated as such, and where one does not, it is not implied.
