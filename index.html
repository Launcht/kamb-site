Kamb Trading (Pty) Ltd

A long-view company that turns real work into businesses worth carrying forward.

Kamb is a South African holding company based in George, Western Cape. It owns four operating companies across AI and automation, marketing and digital development, MVP development, and AI education.

The companies

Each holding is a company in its own right, with its own name, its own market and its own clients. Kamb owns them; it does not run their brands.
