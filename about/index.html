About

The group

Kamb Trading (Pty) Ltd was incorporated in South Africa in 2022 and is based in George, Western Cape. It is founder-owned and independently built.

The group began as a services business and became a holding company as the work produced things worth owning separately. It holds four operating companies today, and the products those companies have built sit under them.

Kamb takes its name from the first initials of the founder's four children. It is a long-horizon company on purpose.

Founder

Tarryn de Jager founded Kamb Trading and each of its operating companies. She sets the group's direction and remains directly involved in the delivery work across the companies.
