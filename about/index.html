About

The group.

Kamb is a privately owned investment and operating group with a long-term view.

Exceptional companies are not discovered fully formed.

Founded in South Africa, Kamb was built around a simple conviction: they are built through clear thinking, decisive action and relentless execution.

The group owns businesses founded internally, alongside companies acquired or backed where there is a strong strategic fit. Across the portfolio, Kamb provides the capital, infrastructure and direction required to build businesses capable of operating independently and scaling over time.

The ambition is not to build one company. It is to build a portfolio of exceptional companies.
