Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party trackers. The only fonts it loads are served by Google Fonts, which receives the request for the font file.

The enquiry form on the contact page is the only place this site collects personal information. It takes your name, your email address, your company if you give one and your message, and sends them to our own database. To stop automated submissions we also record the time the form was open and a one-way hash of your IP address, which cannot be turned back into an address.

We hold what you send only to reply to it and to keep a record of the correspondence. We add you to no marketing list and we pass your details to nobody. To ask what we hold or to have it deleted, write to communications@kamb.co.za.

The Information Officer for Kamb Trading (Pty) Ltd, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act, is the founder, contactable at the same address.
